# Build Log

## Decisions made by Asher (the PM)
- **Product:** Kupa (קופה), a shared budget app for couples. Both partners log expenses by hand in seconds and always see one number: how much is left until the next credit card billing cycle.
- **Primary user:** Noa, 34, the busy partner. She pays often and rarely tracks. Every decision optimizes for her logging speed and her "value moment". Dan (the planner) is present but not designed for.
- **Problem:** Noa can't tell if they are on budget without asking, and logging feels like a chore.
- **Core flow:** open app, tap "+", type amount, tap category (tapping saves, target under 5 seconds), land on home and see "₪1,240 left this cycle".
- **Positioning:** free, private, no bank connection, built around the card billing cycle (vs Riseup: bank sync, subscription). Manual logging is the riskiest assumption, so the MVP measures it.
- **Assumptions measured:** (1) people log if it takes about 5 seconds (`log_duration_ms`, `add_cancel`); (2) "left this cycle" is reason enough to keep logging (expenses per day, `app_open`); (3) billing cycle beats calendar month (cycle start day chosen at setup).
- **Not building:** bank/card sync, kids or more than 2 members, smart/AI categories, charts/reports/insights, income/savings/per-category budgets, notes/receipts/photos/merchants, push/reminders/emails, real accounts, multiple households per device, recurring expenses/splits/debts.
- **Fast lane (deadline):** live on Netlify by Friday Oct 2, 2026 evening. Build Phase 1 and Phase 2 with a local-only repo (LocalRepo, the real app, persisted in `localStorage`) plus the seeded DemoRepo (`?demo=1`). No Supabase, no join/invite until Asher writes "go sync".
- **Partner sync (2026-10-01):** "go sync" for Phase 3 (Supabase, join and invite, realtime), with a safety net: built on a separate `sync` branch tested through a Netlify preview link while the live app stays untouched; hard cutoff Friday Oct 2, 16:00, after which the fallback is the current live app plus the demo "simulate partner expense" button; demo mode stays fully local either way.

## Edge case results
Verified on 2026-10-01 by four audit agents against the live site (headless Chrome with mobile emulation at 390x844 and 360x740, faked clock where a date mattered, a second tab as the stand-in for a partner) plus the unit and integration tests. Three gaps the audit found were fixed the same day and re-verified on the live site.

| # | Case | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Over budget | Pass. Past the budget the number reads "חריגה של ₪50" in red, the per-day part disappears and the bar turns red. Over by less than ₪0.50 shows ₪1, never ₪0. |
| 2 | Budget or cycle day changed by the partner | Not testable in the fast lane (no partner device). Stand-in: a change made in a second tab updates the home screen within milliseconds, without a reload. |
| 3 | Cycle day changed mid-cycle | Pass. The header dates, days left and totals recalculate at once (checked for days 1, 20 and 2), also in a second open tab. |
| 4 | Expense edited into another cycle | Pass after a fix. The row leaves the list and the totals update at once. The audit found the only feedback was a generic "changes saved"; the toast now says "השינויים נשמרו. ההוצאה עברה למחזור קודם.", and an expense added with an earlier date says "במחזור קודם". Such an expense is then reachable only through the CSV export, because previous-cycle navigation was cut. |
| 5 | Future dates | Pass. Blocked in the add sheet (red date chip, message, categories disabled), in the edit sheet (Save disabled) and in the data layer. The real iPhone picker was simulated, not tried on a device. |
| 6 | Invalid amounts | Pass. 0, empty, over ₪100,000 and a third decimal are all blocked in the keypad (add and edit); nothing is saved. |
| 7 | Double tap on a category | Pass after a fix.
